Experimental Strain 01

Platinum Gold Shadow

Male Parent (P1) Full Gold Metallic Y-Linked Golden Body Sheen
Female Parent (P2) Full Black Moscow Maternal Dark Fin Genes

Developed to challenge traditional mono-color strains, the Platinum Gold Shadow blends luminous golden body luster with midnight-black dorsal and caudal fins.

By crossing a pure Full Gold male with a dark Full Black female across three generations (F1 to F3), we successfully isolated a striking bi-color expression. The males feature a mirror-like golden torso paired with jet-black caudal tails, resulting in a dramatic contrast ideal for planted display aquariums.

Yield / Spawn 30-40 Fry
Target pH 7.0 - 7.4

Experimental Strain 02

Crimson Opal Albino

Male Parent (P1) Albino Full Red Ruby Eyes & Deep Crimson
Female Parent (P2) Platinum White Dumbo Opal Scale Iridescence

The Crimson Opal Albino represents an ambitious hybrid attempt to fuse intense ruby pigmentation with pearl iridescence.

Incorporating the autosomal recessive albino trait from the male line alongside the heavy platinum scale coverage of the female parent created a translucent, fiery red body with pastel opal highlights. The result is an ethereal specimen that shimmers red and platinum under overhead aquarium lighting.

Yield / Spawn 25-35 Fry
Target pH 6.8 - 7.2

Experimental Strain 03

Emerald Dragon Mesh

Male Parent (P1) Green Dragon Mosaic Dragon Scale Patterning
Female Parent (P2) Platinum Metal Lace Snakeskin Lattice Fins

Designed for aquascapers seeking intricate pattern density, the Emerald Dragon Mesh combines fine metallic snakeskin webbing with deep emerald green scales.

Inheriting Y-linked dragon motifs from the sire and metallic lattice structures from the dam, this strain exhibits a reticulated mesh across its broad delta tail. Conditioned with live Artemia nauplii and natural tannin-rich water, offspring demonstrate exceptional fin extension and rich color retention.

Yield / Spawn 35-45 Fry
Target pH 7.2 - 7.5
