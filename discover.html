Experimental Strain 01

Platinum Gold Shadow

Male Parent (P1) Full Gold Metallic Y-Linked Golden Sheen
Female Parent (P2) Full Black Moscow Dark Fin Genes

Developed to challenge traditional mono-color strains, the Platinum Gold Shadow blends luminous golden body luster with midnight-black dorsal and caudal fins.

By crossing a pure Full Gold male with a dark Full Black female across three generations (F1 to F3), we successfully isolated a striking bi-color expression.

Yield / Spawn 30-40 Fry
Target pH 7.0 - 7.4
